# About Us

Bass Coast Podiatry opened in July 2007 and has been providing full time Podiatry care since. Bass Coast Podiatry is open 5 days per week and is able to meet any special requirements of those needing urgent foot care. All aspects of the profession are covered and no referral is needed to attend the clinic.

Bass Coast Podiatry services the Department of Veterans Affairs, TAC, and Work Cover and provides on the spot rebates for patients referred from their GP under the EPC program.

## Meet our Staff

#### Luke Zoanetti

Principal Podiatrist

Luke was born and raised in the Bass Coast area. He enjoys all aspects of Podiatry and especially enjoys treating lower leg and foot problems in children, managing biomechanical complaints of the foot. Luke has worked as a casual lecturer for the Podiatry course at Charles Sturt University. Luke enjoys golf and cycling and is an avid downhill snow skier in the winter months.

#### Jennifer Lewis

Principal Podiatrist

Jennifer has worked and lived in the Bass Coast region for the last 15 years. She is passionately committed to the Podiatry profession and enjoys all facets of Podiatry. Jennifer has developed a special interest in high risk and diabetes foot care and assessment. Jennifer enjoys dancing, walking her dog and is also an avid snow skier during the winter months.

#### Alison Kelly

Podiatrist

Alison is highly skilled in diabetes foot care and assessment and biomechanical examination and treatment of the foot. Alison keeps very active outside of work and a highlight of this is completing the Oxfam Trailwalker in 2012, 2013 and 2014.
