Prescription orthoses are used to treat a variety of conditions in the feet and lower limb. Some of these conditions include:

- Shin splints
- Heel pain
- Plantar fasciitis
- Limb length discrepancies
- Lower back pain
- Knee pain
- Forefoot and toe pain
- Ankle pain
- Bunions
- Hammer toes
- Flat feet
- Hallux rigidus
- Hallux limitus
- Morton’s neuroma
- Metatarsalgia
